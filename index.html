Method

Rendering from the game state

Given where a player stands and looks, what everyone in the round is doing, and the map being played, the model renders what that player sees. Map structure, lighting and materials stay consistent as the camera moves through rooms and across levels.

Map-aware

The model knows all seven maps by heart, so walls, doors and skylines stay exactly where the map puts them, however the camera turns.

Full game state

All ten players are part of the scene: who is where, and what they are doing, shows up correctly in every view.

Seven maps

Ancient, Anubis, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage, Nuke and Overpass, at 720p and 32 fps.

Video backbone

Fine-tuned from Cosmos3-Nano with the Wan2.2 VAE; an eight-second window is denoised jointly.

Dataset

Esports World Model Dataset

Replays of real players decoded into multi-view player videos for StarCraft II, Dota 2 and Counter-Strike 2, time-aligned with game actions, engine state and events. Built for action-conditioned world models, multi-view consistency, partial observability and video understanding.
